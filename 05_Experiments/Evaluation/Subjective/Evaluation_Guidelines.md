# Evaluation Guidelines

This document outlines the evaluation criteria for the source separation experiments.

## Vocal_Clarity
- **What is evaluated:** The intelligibility and crispness of the separated vocal stem.
- **High score:** Vocals are perfectly clear, natural, and free from muffling or distortion.
- **Low score:** Vocals are muffled, robotic, distorted, or difficult to understand.

## Vocal_Completeness
- **What is evaluated:** The presence of the entire vocal performance in the vocal stem without missing segments.
- **High score:** All vocal phrases, words, and breaths are present.
- **Low score:** Parts of the vocal performance are missing, dropped, or abruptly cut off.

## Instrument_Preservation
- **What is evaluated:** The retention of traditional folk instruments in the accompaniment stem.
- **High score:** All traditional instruments are clearly audible and maintain their original presence.
- **Low score:** Instruments are missing, significantly attenuated, or completely absent from the mix.

## Timbre_Preservation
- **What is evaluated:** The natural tonal quality and character of the instruments and vocals.
- **High score:** The unique sound (timbre) of the folk instruments and vocals sounds authentic and unaltered.
- **Low score:** The instruments or vocals sound artificial, metallic, synthesized, or unnatural.

## Vocal_Leakage
- **What is evaluated:** The presence of vocals incorrectly bleeding into the accompaniment stem.
- **High score:** No vocal sounds can be heard in the accompaniment stem.
- **Low score:** Significant vocal phrases or words are clearly audible in the accompaniment stem.

## Instrument_Leakage
- **What is evaluated:** The presence of instruments incorrectly bleeding into the vocal stem.
- **High score:** No instruments can be heard in the vocal stem.
- **Low score:** Instruments are loud and clearly audible in the vocal stem, interfering with the vocals.

## Artifacts
- **What is evaluated:** The presence of unwanted noises introduced by the separation algorithm (e.g., watery, metallic, chirping sounds).
- **High score:** No artificial sounds or digital noises are present.
- **Low score:** Frequent, loud, or highly distracting digital artifacts are present throughout the stems.

## Recording_Quality_Impact
- **What is evaluated:** How the original recording quality (e.g., noise, room acoustics) affects the separation.
- **High score:** The model handles the recording quality well without degrading the output.
- **Low score:** The recording's noise or acoustic characteristics severely degrade the separation quality.

## Ornamentation_Preserved
- **What is evaluated:** The retention of delicate vocal or instrumental nuances typical of Indian folk music (e.g., vibrato, glides, microtones).
- **High score:** All subtle ornamentations and nuances are perfectly preserved.
- **Low score:** Ornamentations are smoothed over, missing, or distorted.

## Overall_Quality
- **What is evaluated:** The general, holistic impression of the separation quality for both stems.
- **High score:** Excellent, professional-quality separation highly suitable for further analysis.
- **Low score:** Very poor separation that is unusable for research purposes.

# Rating Scale

Define the scoring system for the evaluation criteria:

* **1** = Very Poor
* **2** = Poor
* **3** = Acceptable
* **4** = Good
* **5** = Excellent

## Confidence

Assign a confidence level to the overall evaluation to reflect the certainty of the assessment. Confidence should be assigned based on how clear the audio is and how easily distinguishable the sources are.

* **High**: The evaluator is completely certain of the assessment (e.g., clear audio, straightforward separation characteristics).
* **Medium**: The evaluator is fairly certain, but some ambiguity exists (e.g., minor overlapping frequencies that are hard to distinguish).
* **Low**: The evaluator is uncertain, usually due to complex, dense mixes, very poor original audio quality, or highly ambiguous separation results.

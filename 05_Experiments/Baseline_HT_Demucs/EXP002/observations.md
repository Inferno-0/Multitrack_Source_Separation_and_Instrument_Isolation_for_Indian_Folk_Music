# EXP002 Observations

## Overview
This document outlines the qualitative evaluation of 10 BIHU folk tradition recordings following batch separation using the pretrained HT-Demucs model. The assessment relies on a manual listening evaluation to document qualitative characteristics.

## Overall Separation Performance
The qualitative evaluation indicates that lead vocals were generally isolated successfully. The accompaniment generally remained complete, and the separation quality remained reasonably consistent across the evaluated recordings.

## Vocal Separation
The vocals remained intelligible throughout the dataset. In most instances, the vocals were generally well isolated from the background mix.

## Vocal Leakage
Minor background singing, whistles, and vocal calls occasionally remained inside the accompaniment stem.

## Instrument Leakage
Flute leakage was the most frequently observed issue within the vocal stem. Additionally, there was occasional leakage of the dhol, taal, and cymbals into the vocal stem.

## Accompaniment Quality
The accompaniment generally remained natural, and important musical components were preserved throughout the dataset.

## Audio Artifacts
Only isolated artifacts were observed during the evaluation. Most recordings remained clean and free from persistent digital distortion.

## Vocal Naturalness
The vocal timbre remained natural, and robotic distortion was uncommon.

## Instrument Preservation
The traditional instruments were generally preserved well, and the accompaniment remained musically coherent.

## Consistent Strengths
- Good vocal isolation
- Natural vocal quality
- Accompaniment preservation
- Low artifact level
- Stable qualitative performance

## Recurring Limitations
- Flute leakage
- Minor secondary vocal leakage
- Occasional percussion leakage
- One outlier recording with lower quality

## Summary
The qualitative evaluation indicates that the pretrained HT-Demucs model provides consistent vocal and accompaniment separation for BIHU folk recordings. While the separation maintains natural vocal timbre and preserves accompaniment coherence with low artifacts, recurring leakage of flute and secondary vocal components was observed.

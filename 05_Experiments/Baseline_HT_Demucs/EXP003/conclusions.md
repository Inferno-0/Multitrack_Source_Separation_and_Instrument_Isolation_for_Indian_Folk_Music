# EXP003 Conclusions

## Overall Finding
HT-Demucs produced generally clean and usable two-stem vocal/accompaniment separation for the three evaluated Baul Geet recordings. This finding is based entirely on the limited qualitative sample and should not be generalized beyond these specific tested tracks.

## Vocal Separation
The evaluation demonstrated clear vocal isolation resulting in understandable lyrics and a natural vocal output. Crucially, there was a lack of noticeable vocal leakage into the accompaniment across the three evaluated tracks.

## Accompaniment Preservation
The accompaniment remained complete and natural. Important instruments were generally preserved throughout the separation process, meaning the No-Vocals outputs did not generally sound hollow or empty.

## Leakage and Failure Case
The principal separation issue was observed in BAUL_016, where the Ektara leaked completely and significantly into the Vocals output. This specific instrument leakage was highly prominent. Despite this leakage, the remaining separation elements were considered usable if the Ektara leakage could be independently addressed.

## Artifacts and Naturalness
No major unnatural or distorted artifacts were observed in the three evaluated recordings. The separated vocals generally retained a natural human character without noticeable robotic degradation.

## Research Implication
Within this three-recording qualitative sample, HT-Demucs produced generally useful two-stem separation on the selected Baul Geet recordings. However, the instrument-specific leakage (such as the Ektara) indicates that the baseline is not sufficient for perfect source isolation. This finding supports continued experimentation across additional Indian folk traditions and later model improvement or fine-tuning, though it does not yet definitively prove that fine-tuning is required solely based on this experiment.

## Limitations
- Only three Baul Geet recordings were evaluated.
- The evaluation was exclusively subjective and manual.
- Objective SDR, SI-SDR, SIR, and SAR evaluation has not yet been included in this document.
- Consequently, these observations should not be treated as a quantitative benchmark.

# EXP004 Observations

## Experiment Information
- **Experiment ID:** EXP004
- **Dataset:** PANDAVANI (Pandavani)
- **Model:** HT-Demucs (htdemucs)
- **Separation type:** Two-stem music source separation (Vocals and No-Vocals)
- **Number of recordings:** 5
- **Evaluation method:** Manual qualitative listening evaluation

## PNDV_009
1. **Vocal Separation Quality:** Vocals were clearly present and intelligible where they occurred. Only minor remnants of non-vocal material were noticed. No parts of the vocals were noticeably missing, suppressed, distorted, or unusually muffled.
2. **Vocal Leakage:** No noticeable vocal leakage was heard in the No-Vocals output.
3. **Instrument Leakage:** Only minor remnants of non-vocal material were noticed in the Vocals output.
4. **Accompaniment Quality:** Instruments/accompaniment were clearly present and usable. No important instruments or musical components appeared noticeably missing or weakened. Volume and character remained reasonably consistent. Vocal suppression was successful without noticeably damaging the accompaniment.
5. **Audio Artifacts:** No obvious artificial artifacts such as metallic sounds, warbling, ringing, or robotic vocal texture were noticed in the Vocals. No obvious separation artifacts were heard in the No-Vocals. No clicks or pops.
6. **Vocal Naturalness:** Vocal level remained consistent, and sections without vocals remained appropriately quiet.
7. **Instrument Preservation:** No important instruments or musical components appeared noticeably missing or weakened.
8. **Continuity and Transition::** Transition and continuity felt natural.
9. **Overall Separation Quality:** Vocals and accompaniment were separated very well, with no noticeable vocal leakage in the No-Vocals output. Both Vocals and No-Vocals pass evaluation. Overall separation is acceptable with very minor issues.
10. **Additional Observations:** No sudden volume/timbre changes. No repeated or missing sounds. No audible problem attributable to the chunking/reconstruction process. No particular problematic section was identified.

## Cross-Recording Observations
The most important cross-recording observation is consistency. Across all five Pandavani recordings, vocal separation was consistently clean and acceptable. Only minor non-vocal remnants were noticed in the Vocals outputs, and no noticeable vocal leakage was heard in the No-Vocals outputs. The accompaniment remained usable and intact. No major artificial artifacts were noticed. No clicks, pops, repetitions, missing sections, or abrupt changes were identified. No audible chunk-boundary or reconstruction artifacts were identified. The outputs showed no major qualitative change from one recording to another.
